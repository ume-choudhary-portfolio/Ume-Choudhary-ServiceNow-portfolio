WORK ORDER LIFECYCLE

From request to controlled execution.

01 · IntakeCapture service request, site, asset, priority and required information.
02 · ClassifyCategorise work type and define ownership, priority and service level.
03 · AssignRoute to the appropriate resource/team based on location, skill or service rule.
04 · CloseComplete work, capture outcome, confirm service evidence and update status.

SCHEDULING & DISPATCH

Right work, right resource, right sequence.

DemandOpen work by priority, location and due date.
CapacityAvailable technician/resource capacity and constraints.
AssignmentAllocate work using skill, geography, urgency and service rules.
DispatchSequence execution and keep operational hand-offs visible.

SLA & ESCALATION

Make service risk visible before it becomes failure.

GreenWork is within service target.
AmberThreshold approaching; review ownership and next action.
RedSLA at risk or breached; trigger escalation.
LearnUse recurring exceptions to improve routing, capacity or process design.

MAINTENANCE & ASSET SERVICE

Connect the work to the asset and its dependencies.

AssetIdentify the affected asset/service and operational context.
MaintenanceDefine required task, priority and maintenance workflow.
PartsCheck inventory/parts dependency where relevant.
TraceabilityCapture action, status, evidence and service history.
